# Kemira — My GEO Research and WordPress Experience

## Why I did this

I wanted to understand how AI assistants describe Kemira and whether the information they give is accurate and up to date.

I also wanted to show how I could help the Communications team with practical, low-cost solutions.

## What I tested

I researched Kemira using ChatGPT and asked Claude and Gemini questions about:

- What Kemira does and who its customers are.
- Its sustainability work and water-treatment solutions.
- Whether the information is accurate and up to date.
- What information is missing or difficult to verify.
- Which sources the AI assistants use.

I compared their answers with information published on Kemira's official website.

## What I found

Both Claude and Gemini explained Kemira's water-treatment business quite well.

But I also found an important mistake.

**Gemini described Kemira using its old business structure.** It mentioned two main business areas instead of the three business units introduced in January 2025:

- Water Solutions
- Packaging & Hygiene Solutions
- Fiber Essentials

Claude used the newer structure but could not verify some sustainability details.

I also noticed that some AI answers included figures without clear links to the exact sources.

This showed me that AI assistants can give useful answers while still using outdated or incomplete information.

## How I could help Kemira

I have hands-on experience working with WordPress, PHP and website maintenance.

If I had access to Kemira's WordPress dashboard, I could help the team:

- Update outdated company information on important pages.
- Improve page structure and make answers easier for AI assistants to find.
- Add useful FAQs and structured data where appropriate.
- Fix website issues and check for broken or outdated content.
- Build simple custom PHP plugins and automations instead of buying expensive tools when a small custom solution is enough.
- Monitor changes and regularly check whether AI assistants describe Kemira correctly.

I would test changes first and work with the Communications team before publishing them.

## My real WordPress experience

I previously worked with WordPress at **Coreorient**, where I helped maintain and improve the company's website.

One of the problems I worked on was a large amount of spam in the WordPress dashboard. I cleaned it up and helped improve the website's maintenance and SEO-related housekeeping.

I also built custom solutions using PHP and WordPress plugins instead of purchasing paid plugins for every task.

This helped the company avoid unnecessary software costs.

That experience taught me something important: sometimes a small technical fix can make a real difference to a company.

## What I learned

My main takeaway is that GEO is not just about using AI tools. It is about keeping information accurate, clear and easy to find.

I enjoy finding practical problems and solving them with WordPress, PHP, JavaScript and automation.

I would like to bring that same approach to Kemira: start with a real problem, build a simple solution, test it and improve it.
