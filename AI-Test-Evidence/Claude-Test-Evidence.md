# Claude Test Evidence — Kemira GEO Research

**Test date:** October 2026  
**AI assistant:** Claude

## Why I Tested Claude

I wanted to see how Claude describes Kemira and whether its information is accurate, current and supported by sources.

I reviewed Claude's answer about Kemira's business, sustainability and water-treatment solutions.

## What Claude Got Right

Claude correctly identified Kemira as a Finnish chemistry company serving water-intensive industries.

It also identified Kemira's three current business units:

- **Water Solutions**
- **Packaging & Hygiene Solutions**
- **Fiber Essentials**

These business units were introduced on January 1, 2025.

Claude also explained several water-treatment applications, including:

- Coagulants and flocculants.
- Sludge dewatering.
- Phosphorus removal.
- Activated carbon for removing certain pollutants.

## What I Found Interesting

Claude provided more detail about Kemira's company structure and sustainability reporting than Gemini's company overview.

It also identified information it could not verify, including:

- The calculation method behind Kemira's estimated 21 billion m³ water-treatment impact.
- Some financial and business-unit details.
- The evidence supporting certain older market-leadership claims.

I think this is useful because an AI assistant should explain what it knows and what remains uncertain.

## An Important Verification Gap

Claude wrote:

> I found no independent verification of them.

This referred to Kemira's sustainability figures.

However, not finding independent verification does not prove that none exists.

In a separate Gemini response, Gemini mentioned that EY provided limited assurance for Kemira's 2025 sustainability reporting.

That claim should be checked against the original assurance statement. Limited assurance of a report also does not automatically verify every individual sustainability claim.

## Why This Matters for GEO

Claude gave a more up-to-date description of Kemira's business structure, but some of its sustainability information still needed further checking.

This shows that even a detailed AI response should not be treated as fully verified without reviewing the original sources.

## What I Would Do

I would help make important company information clear, current and supported by accessible sources.

I would also repeat the same questions across different AI assistants over time and compare their answers with official information.
