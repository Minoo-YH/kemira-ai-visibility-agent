# Gemini Test Evidence — Kemira GEO Research

**Test date:** October 2026  
**AI assistant:** Gemini

## Why I Tested Gemini

I wanted to see whether Gemini provides accurate and up-to-date information about Kemira, its business and its water-treatment solutions.

I reviewed two Gemini responses: one company overview and one separate follow-up about sustainability claims.

## My Main Finding

**Gemini described Kemira using an outdated business structure.**

It listed two business areas:

- "Industry & Flow (Water Treatment)"
- "Pulp & Paper"

However, Kemira introduced three business units on January 1, 2025:

- **Water Solutions**
- **Packaging & Hygiene Solutions**
- **Fiber Essentials**

The previous business units were called "Industry & Water" and "Pulp & Paper". Gemini also used "Industry & Flow", which was not the official previous name.

**Official source:** [Kemira's organizational announcement](https://www.kemira.com/news-and-stories/newsroom/releases/kemira-publishes-comparison-figures-to-reflect-the-new-organizational-structure-with-three-business-units/)

## What Gemini Got Right

Gemini correctly described Kemira as a Finnish chemical company serving water-intensive industries.

It also gave useful explanations of:

- Coagulants and flocculants.
- Sludge treatment.
- Water reuse and recycling.
- Digital water-treatment monitoring.

## What Needs Verification

Gemini mentioned possible energy savings of up to 50% and biogas increases of up to two times without direct sources for those exact figures.

In a separate follow-up, Gemini said that EY provided limited assurance for Kemira's sustainability reporting and that Kemira received an A- CDP rating.

I would verify these claims against the original reports before presenting them as confirmed facts.

## Why This Matters

An AI assistant can explain a company's products correctly while still giving outdated information about its current business structure.

For a Communications team, this is a useful GEO issue to monitor.

## What I Would Do

I would first check whether Kemira's official website already explains its current business units clearly.

If improvements are needed, I could help update the content and page structure, with approval.

Then I would repeat the AI tests to see whether the answers become more accurate.
