# ChatGPT Research Summary — Kemira GEO Project

**Research period:** October 2026  
**AI assistant:** ChatGPT  
**Purpose:** Initial research and preparation for a small GEO comparison.

## Why I Used ChatGPT

I wanted to understand Kemira's business, water-treatment solutions and sustainability work before comparing information from other AI assistants.

I used ChatGPT to explore what information would be important to check and how to approach the research.

## What I Researched

My initial research focused on:

- Kemira's company information and business structure.
- Water-treatment products and applications.
- Sustainability information and supporting sources.
- Possible outdated or unclear information in AI-generated answers.
- Practical ways to improve website content for AI-powered search.

## What I Learned

One important lesson was that an AI-generated answer is not automatically correct.

Even when an answer sounds detailed, it may include older company information, unsupported figures or missing context.

This is why I compared the later Claude and Gemini answers with Kemira's official information.

## How I Used This Research

ChatGPT helped me organize the questions and identify areas worth investigating.

I then reviewed responses from Claude and Gemini.

The clearest finding was in Gemini's company overview, which used an outdated two-part business structure instead of Kemira's three current business units.

## Limitations

This document is a summary of my initial research process.

It is not a complete ChatGPT conversation transcript or a standardized test using the same prompts as Claude and Gemini.

I have not measured AI search rankings, citation frequency or changes in Kemira's visibility.
